Research note · Global weather forecasting

KAI-α: lightweight global weather forecasting

Muhammad Ahmed · KAIST MetaEarth Research Center
2024–Present · In progress

I co-develop a compact convolutional model for medium-range weather forecasting and build the systems used to train and evaluate it.

The work

I co-developed KAI-α and built its training and inference pipelines. I also built a modular evaluation framework to compare architectures through a shared workflow.

The project reports competitive medium-range forecasting skill with approximately four times fewer parameters than GraphCast and FourCastNet. The visual comparisons below show particular forecast fields and their errors; they are not a substitute for aggregate benchmark scores.

Representing
the globe

The globe and unfolded HEALPix faces show how a spherical weather field can be organized into local patches for computation.

10-day rollout

Each prediction becomes the input to the next step. This ten-day excerpt follows the KAI local-normalized configuration (13.46 million parameters, HEALPix nside 64), initialized on 1 January 2018.
