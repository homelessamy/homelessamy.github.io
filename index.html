Development and Evaluation of Graph Neural Network Based Climate Downscaling Methods

Preprint · draft

Muhammad Ahmed

Undergraduate Research Program · Spring 2025

Graph-based climate downscaling for ERA5 over South Asia, with a multi-scale graph reasoning module and a spectral–structural loss. Reaches 60–85% RMSE reduction across winds, temperature, humidity and radiative fluxes, and about 84% for surface pressure. Spectral errors stay within ±0.2% across 90% of wavenumbers.
